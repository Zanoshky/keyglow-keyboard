01install

Get Keyglow on Google Play. You pay once: 2.49 USD or EUR, and 1.49 in many countries. There are no ads, no subscription and no account. Keyglow runs on Android 7 or newer, on phones and tablets, in portrait and landscape.

02turn it on and pick it

Android keeps every keyboard switched off until you turn it on, so Keyglow starts with two steps under set up. Each one ticks itself once it is done.

  1. 1. Turn on Keyglow opens Android's list of keyboards. Switch Keyglow on. Android shows a warning that a keyboard can see what you type; that is shown for every keyboard. Keyglow has no internet permission, so what you type cannot leave your phone.
  2. 2. Switch to Keyglow opens Android's keyboard picker. Pick Keyglow.

Then type in the try it field just below. The settings screen is drawn in the keyboard's own colours, so it previews your theme too.

To open the settings later, open the Keyglow app, or tap the gear in the tools row above the keys.

03switch keyboards

  • Hold the space bar to open Android's keyboard picker.
  • With two or more languages on, holding the globe key does the same.
  • Many phones also show a small keyboard button in the navigation bar while you type.

To go back to your old keyboard, pick it in the same place. Keyglow keeps your settings and words.

04the keys and pages

Your layout, with shift, delete, ?123, comma, space, full stop and enter. The enter key shows what the field asks for.
?123
Numbers and symbols. =\< opens a second symbols page. abc goes back to letters.
number pad
Shown by itself in a field that asks for a number.
phone pad
Shown by itself in a field that asks for a phone number.
long press
Hold a letter for its number and its accents. Hold a symbol for its relatives, like € £ ¥ on $.
shift
Tap once for one capital, tap twice for caps lock. A capital comes by itself at the start of a sentence, if Capital letter at the start of a sentence is on.
emoji key
Next to space, if Emoji key next to space is on.

06layout

Under layout: Automatic, QWERTY, QWERTZ, AZERTY, Dvorak or Colemak.

Automatic takes the board each language is usually typed on: QWERTZ for German, AZERTY for French, the Turkish board for Turkish. Switch language with the globe key and the board follows.

07look and themes

Under look, pick one of three:

Match my launcher
With Hyprtile or Phosphor as your home screen, Keyglow takes the theme you use there. Change the theme in the launcher and the keyboard follows. The line shows which launcher and theme it found.
Tiled
Windows with gaps like Hyprtile: tiles with gradient borders.
Terminal
Glowing text like Phosphor: a console panel with a double frame and a title, bracketed keys, a blinking block cursor on space and a CRT beam rolling down.

Below it are 84 themes: Hyprtile's 49 and Phosphor's 35. Each chip is drawn in its own colours. Tap one to use it; that also switches the look to match.

If the line reads update Hyprtile to share its theme or the same for Phosphor, your launcher is an older version. Update it from Google Play and Keyglow picks up the theme.

08lighting

Every key is lit from below in its own colour, like an RGB keyboard, with LED strips along the edges.

light
Off, Steady, Flow in the theme's colours, Rainbow flow or Breathe.
which way it flows
Left to right, Right to left, Up, Down, Diagonal, Circles out from the middle, Circles in to the middle, Rotate clockwise or Rotate anticlockwise.
speed
A slider from 1 to 10.
brightness
A slider from 20 to 100 percent.
when you press a key
Off, Light up the key, A small ring round it or A ring across the whole board.
White ripples
Presses light up in white instead of the light's colour.

The lights hold still when animations are off in Android's settings.

09suggestions and autocorrect

While you type, the strip above the keys shows three words. The middle one is what space will put in. When it would correct what you typed, it is lit. The word exactly as you typed it shows in quotes, so you can always keep it.

  • Suggestions and autocorrect use only the language you are on. The globe key switches it.
  • Autocorrect: Off, Mild: only clear slips, Normal or Strong.
  • Delete right after a correction takes it back, and the word you typed is kept. Keyglow remembers that and does not make that fix again. Tapping your own word over a lit fix does the same.
  • Keyglow learns your own words and which word follows which, for next word suggestions. It also learns where your thumb tends to land on each key, so the slips you often make cost less.
  • Hold a suggestion to never see it again. It goes to never suggested on the your words screen, where you can allow it again.

10glide typing

Slide your finger across the letters of a word and lift. With Show the glide line on, a line follows your finger. Turn it all off with Glide typing: slide across the letters.

11the tools row

The emoji panel.

The clipboard panel.

The edit panel.

Just after you copy something, a paste chip with the start of that text takes the tools' place. Tap it to paste.

12emoji

Open it with the emoji key next to space or from the tools row. Tabs along the top jump between groups, with your recent ones first. The search key at the bottom finds emoji by name. Hold an emoji that comes in skin tones to pick one; Keyglow remembers your pick. abc goes back to letters.

13clipboard

With Keep what you copy for an hour on, text you copy shows here for an hour. Tap a clip to paste it. Pin a clip to keep it; the bin deletes one; clear removes every clip you did not pin.

Clips stay in memory, never in a file, unless you pin them. Nothing copied in a password field, or marked sensitive by the app, is kept.

14edit panel

Arrows, start and end of the line, select (turn it on, then move to select), all, copy, cut, paste, undo, redo and delete.

16your words screen

Under your words in settings, tap Your words, shortcuts and blocked words. All of this stays on your phone.

add a word
Teach it a name or a word before you type it.
shortcuts
Add a shortcut and the text it stands for, or delete one.
learned words
Every word it learned, with how often you used it. Find a word filters the list; forget removes one.
never suggested
Words you held to block. allow lets one back.

Back in settings:

  • Learn the words you use turns learning off or on.
  • Back up your words to a file saves your words, shortcuts and blocked words as a file you choose, such as keyglow-words.json.
  • Restore your words from a file adds them back, on this phone or a new one.
  • Forget everything Keyglow learned asks for a second tap, then clears it all.

17one handed and split

One handed (phones): Off, Keys on the left or Keys on the right. You can also turn it on from the tools row. A column beside the keys has swap, to move them to the other side, and full, to go back to full width.

Split the keys on a wide screen breaks the board into two halves for two thumbs on a tablet or in landscape.

18number and terminal rows

Under keys:

  • Number row: the digits above the letters.
  • Terminal row: esc, tab, ctrl, alt and arrows, plus / and -, for ssh and editors. Tap ctrl or alt, then a letter, to send that key together with it.

Both rows are on by default on a tablet and off on a phone.

Off, Light, Medium or Strong.

Keyglow has no key sounds on purpose: a sound on every key gets tiring fast. It uses a soft vibration instead.

20incognito and privacy

Tap incognito in the tools row and Keyglow learns nothing until you tap it again. The strip says incognito: nothing you type is learned.

  • No internet permission: Android does not let Keyglow reach the network. Its one permission is vibration, for the key buzz.
  • No ads, no account, no analytics.
  • The words it learns, your shortcuts and pinned clips sit in its own private files on your phone, and go when you clear them or remove the app. If Android backup is on, Android may copy them to your own Google account; you can also back them up to a file.
  • Nothing is learned in password fields, in fields that ask for no learning, or while incognito is on.
  • The theme comes straight from Hyprtile or Phosphor on the same phone.

The full privacy policy is on the website.

21accessibility

  • TalkBack: every key is its own spoken target. Touch to hear it, lift to type.
  • Keys are never smaller than 48 dp, in landscape too.
  • Labels fit at 200 percent text size.
  • Haptics instead of sounds, from off to strong.
  • The lights hold still when animations are off in Android's settings.
